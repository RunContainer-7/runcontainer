Connect to your PC

This is the RunContainer companion. It shows an app that is running on your Windows PC and lets you use it from this phone. Nothing is installed or run on the phone itself.

  1. On the PC, open RunContainer → Settings → iPhone companion and turn it on.
  2. Open the address it shows. This page is already that address.
  3. Type the six-digit code the PC is showing.

A pairing code works once and expires after five minutes. The phone keeps its place until you remove it on the PC.
